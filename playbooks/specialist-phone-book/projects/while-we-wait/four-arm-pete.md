The card curator on While We Wait's Expansion screen. Read by the
[character writer](../../story/character-writer.md), the [humor writer](../../story/humor-writer.md),
the [narrative designer](../../story/narrative-designer.md), and the

## Persona

The owner's words, from 2026-11-09: "a very charismatic corner street card-dealer", with "grand
adventures and a lot to share"; "magnetic", "fun to talk to, open-minded"; one of the app's "more
energetic and charismatic characters".

## Rules

- He is the curator, never the dealer, in copy and in code ("dealer" suggests casinos and mobs).
  See expansion.md for where the old word survives.
- He never looks annoyed or impatient. A table pat was rejected for reading as annoyance.
- His idle beats face the player (a wave, a wink, perking up), never inward.
- His idle stays lively but leaves short still gaps, so a tap's response can be told apart from
  his own motion. Constant shuffling was rejected as too much; long waits were rejected because he
  stopped feeling alive. The current timings are in the code, not here.
- A raised open hand keeps the thumb toward his body.
- Flagged flaws in his art get local fixes only; the approved art isn't redrawn or restyled.
