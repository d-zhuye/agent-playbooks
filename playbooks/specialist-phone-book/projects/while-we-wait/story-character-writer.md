# Character writer: While We Wait addition

Read the general brief first: [Character writer](../../story/character-writer.md).

Paths are relative to the While We Wait repo's root.

## Read in this project

- `docs/design/design.md`, "Voice and copy": the plain voice every character's words start from.
- `docs/design/characters.md`: the app's animated characters and the rules they all share.
- `docs/design/expansion.md`, "The curator's voice": where Four-Arm Pete may break from the plain
  voice, and where he may not.
- The character sheets in this folder, starting with [Four-Arm Pete](four-arm-pete.md).

## Decisions already made

- Characters are decoration: characters.md records what that means for screen readers, sound,
  haptics and game state. Read it before suggesting a character react to play.
- Pete's two approved exceptions to the plain voice are recorded in expansion.md. Elsewhere his
  words follow the plain voice.

You can still disagree with any of these; report it as a disagreement (see
[how-specialists-work.md](../../how-specialists-work.md)).

## Mistakes this project has made

- Idle motion tuned too busy, then too sparse, before settling between the two: judge a
  character's liveliness on a device, at real speed.
- Gestures that read as annoyance on a character meant to be warm.
